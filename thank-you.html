Premium VPN Setup & Savings Guide

Get protected in about 10 minutes, and never pay full price.

1. Pick the right VPN in 30 seconds

  • Just want the best all-rounder? NordVPN.
  • Lots of devices in the house? Surfshark or Private Internet Access (unlimited devices).
  • Speed and streaming first? ExpressVPN.
  • Privacy first, or want to try free? Proton VPN.
  • Nervous about committing? CyberGhost (45-day refund on longer plans).

2. How to never pay full price

  • Buy the long plan. One- and two-year plans usually cost a fraction of the monthly price per month.
  • Wait for the big sales if you can. The deepest discounts usually come around Black Friday and Cyber Monday (late November), New Year, and summer.
  • Check the renewal price. Intro prices often go up at renewal. Put a reminder in your phone one week before the renewal date so you can decide whether to switch or renegotiate.
  • Look for bundles. Many plans include extras like a password manager or antivirus. If you'd pay for those anyway, a bundle can save money.
  • Try turning off auto-renew. Log into your account and start to cancel auto-renew. Some providers offer a discount to keep you.

3. Use the money-back guarantee the right way

  1. Write down the date you bought it and the refund deadline (30 days for most providers, 45 for CyberGhost's longer plans).
  2. Test it in the first week on your phone, laptop, TV, and home Wi-Fi.
  3. If it doesn't work for you, contact support by live chat and ask for a refund before the deadline. Keep the chat transcript.
  4. Buying through the app store (Apple or Google) can mean different refund rules. Buying on the provider's website is usually simpler.

4. Setup, device by device

iPhone / iPad

Install the provider's app from the App Store and log in. Tap "Allow" when iOS asks to add a VPN configuration. Then turn on "Connect on demand" or "Auto-connect" in the app's settings.

Android

Install the app from Google Play and log in. Accept the connection request. Then go to phone Settings → Network → VPN → tap the gear next to your VPN → turn on "Always-on VPN."

Windows / Mac

Download the app from the provider's website (not a third-party site). Log in, then turn on "Launch at startup" and "Auto-connect."

Smart TV / Fire TV / Android TV

Search the TV's app store for your VPN. If it's not there, many providers have a Smart DNS feature you set up in the TV's network settings. Check your provider's help center.

Router (whole-home protection)

Only some routers support VPNs. Look up "[your router model] + [your VPN] setup" in your provider's help center. This protects every device on your Wi-Fi, including game consoles.

5. The 5-minute settings checklist

  • ☐ Kill switch ON: blocks the internet if the VPN drops, so nothing leaks.
  • ☐ Auto-connect ON: especially on untrusted Wi-Fi.
  • ☐ Protocol set to Automatic or WireGuard/Lightway/NordLynx: the fastest modern options.
  • ☐ Ad/malware blocker ON: NordVPN Threat Protection, Surfshark CleanWeb, Proton NetShield, or PIA MACE.
  • ☐ Leak test: connect, then visit your provider's leak test page or search "DNS leak test." Your real location and internet provider should not show.

6. Streaming, travel & public Wi-Fi tips

  • If a streaming app shows an error, switch to another server in the same country, then clear the app's cache or reinstall it.
  • Use a VPN on hotel, airport, and café Wi-Fi every time.
  • Before traveling, install and log into the VPN at home. Some countries block VPN websites.
  • Slow speeds? Choose a server closer to you and switch to the fastest protocol.
  • Banking sites sometimes flag VPN connections. Connect to a server in your own country.
