Questions people ask

Do I really need a VPN?

A VPN encrypts your connection. That matters most on public Wi-Fi, when you travel, or when you don't want your internet provider to see which sites you visit.

Which VPN is best overall?

For most people, NordVPN is the best all-round pick. Surfshark and Private Internet Access are strong choices if you want unlimited devices on one plan.

Can I get my money back?

Yes. Every provider here offers a money-back guarantee. CyberGhost's is 45 days on longer plans, and the others offer 30 days.

Is there a free option?

Proton VPN has a free plan for one device that connects to a limited set of countries. It's the best free option we know of.
